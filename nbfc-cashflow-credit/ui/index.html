Vendor Credit Console
SYNTHETIC DATA checking service…

Cash-flow credit for vendors with no credit history

Four focused agents help an NBFC assess street vendors and small shops from their daily UPI receipts. They support decisions; they do not make them. The lender decides, and a person reviews every case that is not a clear approve.

Everything here runs on synthetic data. No result on this page is real-world accuracy. The model must be validated on a lender's anonymised past loans before any use.

How a case flows

1. ConsentVendor agrees to share UPI receipts. No consent, no score.

→
4. Lender decidesReview and decline bands always go to a person.
→
5. Early warningWeekly checks after disbursal; a person follows up.
