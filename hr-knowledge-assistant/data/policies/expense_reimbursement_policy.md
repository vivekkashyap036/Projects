# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy — Nimbus Technologies

## 1. General Rules
- All business expenses must be pre-approved by the reporting manager.
- Claims must be submitted on Nimbus People within **30 days** of incurring the expense, with original receipts or invoices.
- Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## 2. Travel
- Domestic flights: economy class for all employees. Bookings must be made through the company travel desk at least **14 days** in advance where possible.
- Train travel: AC 2-tier for all employees.
- Local commute for client meetings: actual cab fare, with the trip receipt.

## 3. Hotel and Per Diem
- Hotel limits: up to **INR 6,000 per night** in metro cities and INR 4,000 per night in other cities.
- Daily per diem for meals during business travel: **INR 1,200 per day** in India.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
- Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades not approved in advance are not reimbursed.

## 5. Client Entertainment
- Client entertainment expenses require approval from the department head and must list the attendees.
