# Code of Conduct and Workplace Policies — Nimbus Technologies

## 1. Respectful Workplace
- Nimbus has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bullying of any kind.
- Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the **Internal Committee (IC)** under the POSH Act. Write to ic@nimbus.example. Complaints are kept confidential.

## 2. Whistleblower Policy
- Employees can report unethical behaviour, fraud or policy violations anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at ethics@nimbus.example.
- No employee will face retaliation for reporting concerns in good faith.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
- Employees must disclose any outside employment, freelance work or financial interest in a vendor or competitor to HR.
- Moonlighting (working for another organisation) is **not permitted** without written approval from HR.

## 4. Information Security
- Do not share passwords, customer data or source code outside the company.
- Report lost laptops or phones to the IT helpdesk within **24 hours**.

## 5. Dress Code
- Business casual on regular days. Formal attire is expected in client meetings.

## 6. Performance Reviews
- Performance reviews are conducted **twice a year**, in April and October.
- Salary revisions and promotions are announced once a year, effective **1 April**.

## 7. Resignation and Exit
- Employees must serve the notice period (60 days after confirmation). Notice period buy-out is possible with manager approval.
- The full and final settlement is processed within **45 days** of the last working day.
