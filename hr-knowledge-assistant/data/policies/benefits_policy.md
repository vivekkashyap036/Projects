# Employee Benefits — Nimbus Technologies

## 1. Health Insurance
- All employees are covered under a group medical insurance policy with a sum insured of **INR 5,00,000 per family per year**.
- Coverage includes employee, spouse, up to 2 children and either parents or parents-in-law.
- Employees may buy a top-up cover of an additional INR 10,00,000 at a subsidised premium during the annual enrolment window in **April**.
- Cashless hospitalisation is available at network hospitals. For reimbursement claims, submit documents to the insurer within **30 days** of discharge.

## 2. Life and Accident Insurance
- Term life insurance cover of **3 times the annual CTC**.
- Personal accident cover of INR 25,00,000.

## 3. Retirement Benefits
- Provident Fund (PF): the company contributes **12% of basic salary** to PF.
- Gratuity is paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act after **5 years** of continuous service.

## 4. Learning and Development
- Each employee has an annual learning budget of **INR 50,000** for courses, certifications and conferences, subject to manager approval.
- Certification exam fees (for example, cloud certifications) are reimbursed on passing the exam.

## 5. Wellness
- Free annual health check-up for employees.
- Access to the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) with **6 free confidential counselling sessions** per year.
- Gym membership reimbursement of up to **INR 1,000 per month**.

## 6. Meal and Commute
- Free lunch is provided in the office cafeteria on working days.
- Night shift employees are provided company transport after 8:00 PM.
