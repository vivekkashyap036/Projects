# Leave Policy — Nimbus Technologies

Effective: 1 January 2026. Applies to all full-time employees in India.

## 1. Annual (Earned) Leave
- Every full-time employee is entitled to **24 days of earned leave** per calendar year.
- Earned leave accrues monthly at the rate of **2 days per month**.
- Employees may carry forward a maximum of **10 unused earned leave days** into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December.
- Earned leave must be applied for at least **7 days in advance** through the HR portal (Nimbus People), except in emergencies.
- Up to **15 days** of accumulated earned leave can be encashed at the time of resignation, calculated on basic salary.

## 2. Sick Leave
- Employees receive **12 days of paid sick leave** per year.
- Sick leave does not carry forward and cannot be encashed.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding **2 consecutive days**.

## 3. Casual Leave
- Employees receive **6 days of casual leave** per year for personal matters.
- Casual leave cannot be combined with earned leave to exceed 10 continuous days.

## 4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity leave: **26 weeks of paid leave** for the first two children, and 12 weeks thereafter, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Paternity leave: **10 working days of paid leave**, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.
- Adoption leave: **12 weeks** for the primary caregiver.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
- Up to **5 days of paid bereavement leave** in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law).

## 6. Public Holidays
- Nimbus observes **12 public holidays** each year. The list is published on Nimbus People every December.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be used within 60 days.

## 7. Leave Without Pay
- Leave without pay (LWP) may be granted for up to 30 days with approval from the manager and the HR Business Partner.
