# New Joiner Onboarding Guide — Nimbus Technologies

Welcome to Nimbus! This guide covers your first 90 days.

## Before Day 1
- You will receive your offer letter, the Nimbus People login and a laptop shipping notification.
- Upload the following documents on Nimbus People before joining: PAN card, Aadhaar card, educational certificates, relieving letters from previous employers and a cancelled cheque for salary credit.

## Day 1
- Report to the reception at **9:30 AM** at the Bengaluru office (Embassy Tech Village, Tower B, 5th floor), or join the virtual induction if you are a remote joiner.
- Your IT setup (laptop, email, Slack, VPN) is completed by the IT helpdesk. Raise issues at it-help@nimbus.example.
- Collect your ID card from the Admin desk.

## Week 1
- Attend the **New Joiner Induction** sessions covering company values, the Code of Conduct, information security and the POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) policy.
- Complete the mandatory e-learning modules on Nimbus Learn within **14 days**: Information Security Awareness, Data Privacy, and Code of Conduct.
- You will be assigned an **onboarding buddy** from your team who helps you settle in.

## Probation
- All new employees are on probation for **6 months**.
- A probation review is conducted by your manager in month 5. On successful completion you receive a confirmation letter.
- The notice period during probation is **30 days**; after confirmation it is **60 days**.

## First 90 Days
- Set your goals with your manager on Nimbus People within the first **30 days**.
- Check in with your HR Business Partner (HRBP) at day 30, 60 and 90.
