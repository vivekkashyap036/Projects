# Hybrid Work and Work-From-Home Policy — Nimbus Technologies

## 1. Hybrid Model
- Nimbus follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office **3 days per week** (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are anchor days) and may work remotely on the other 2 days.
- Teams may agree on different anchor days with written approval from their department head.

## 2. Full Remote Work
- Fully remote arrangements are granted only for medical reasons or exceptional circumstances, for a maximum of **3 months at a time**, and require approval from the manager and HR.
- Employees in their probation period (first 6 months) are not eligible for fully remote work.

## 3. Work From Anywhere
- Employees may work from a location other than their home city for up to **20 working days per year**, provided they remain in India and have reliable internet.
- Working from outside India requires prior approval from HR and the Legal team due to tax and compliance implications.

## 4. Home Office Allowance
- New joiners receive a one-time **home office allowance of INR 15,000** to buy a chair, desk or monitor. Claims must be submitted with invoices within 90 days of joining.
- A monthly **internet reimbursement of up to INR 1,500** is paid on submission of bills.

## 5. Expectations
- Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of **11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST**.
- Company laptops must be connected to the VPN when accessing internal systems.
